Tianum Networth Managers Pvt. Ltd. follows a research-driven approach to wealth management. We help clients evaluate investment opportunities based on their financial goals, existing portfolio, risk profile and time horizon, with solutions spanning relevant investment categories such as equities, mutual funds, fixed income, PMS and AIFs, subject to eligibility and applicable service arrangements.

Wealth management can be relevant for High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNIs), Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs), family offices, professionals and individual investors. The appropriate approach depends on each client's financial objectives, investment horizon, risk profile and requirements.

We begin by understanding your existing investments, assets, liabilities, financial goals, risk comfort and financial commitments. We then evaluate suitable investment options, with portfolio monitoring and rebalancing considered as circumstances and goals change.

Depending on your requirements and eligibility, options may include equities, mutual funds and SIPs, fixed income, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), Portfolio Management Services (PMS) and Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs). The suitability of any option depends on its features and your individual circumstances.
